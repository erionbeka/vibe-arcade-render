PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

What we store. When you register we store your username and a hashed password (we never see or store your actual password). If you upload games we store those files, your descriptions, screenshots and tags. Comments, ratings, likes, and play counts are also stored.

Cookies. We use a single login session cookie so you stay logged in. It contains no personal data beyond an opaque session id. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Log data. The server keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and abuse prevention.

What is public. Your username, uploaded games, comments, ratings and likes are visible to other visitors. Do not put personal information in public fields.

Deletion. You can delete your own games and comments at any time. To delete your account entirely, contact the site owner.

Third parties. The site loads two fonts from Google Fonts; Google may log those font requests. No other third-party services receive your data.
